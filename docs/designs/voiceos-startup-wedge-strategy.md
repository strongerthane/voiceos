## Problem Statement

VoiceOS has been built as a full enterprise platform (analytics, workflows, smart plugins, search, scheduling, TTS, file organization) but has zero paying users. The product was built before validating that anyone would pay — "platform vision before wedge."

## Demand Evidence

None. Pre-product stage — no paying users, no one using it this week without being asked. Confirmed by founder: "No. Just finished development."

## Status Quo

Founder's dad — an investor managing 3 teams — handles email manually while doing investment work. When an email pops up, he reads it and responds, interrupting his primary task. He sometimes misses profitable margins due to these interruptions. He currently pays a minimum of $16/month for something (unclear what).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

Target user: founder's dad (investor, manages 3 teams, into investing). Real pain: missing profitable margins when email interrupts investment work.

Narrowest wedge: voice-handling email so he can stay focused on his primary investing work. Smallest version: a single-purpose tool that reads incoming email aloud and lets him dictate replies by voice, without leaving his investment workflow.

## Constraints

- Pre-product, zero paying users
- Competitor with 80% market share in the space (market exists, VoiceOS share unknown)
- Full enterprise platform already built but unvalidated
- $16/month price reference point

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Minimal viable wedge

Strip VoiceOS down to voice email handling for dad's workflow. Hide other 6 feature categories. Ship this week.
- Effort: S | Risk: Low | Pros: fastest to validate, directly tests if dad pays | Cons: leaves existing code unused, narrows market temporarily
- Reuses: voice_io.py, voice_feedback.py, app_actions.py

### Approach B: Ideal architecture — progressive disclosure platform

Keep the full enterprise platform but add onboarding that routes new users to their #1 workflow first, with analytics tracking which feature each user engages with most. Double down on the winning wedge over time.
- Effort: L | Risk: Med | Pros: preserves all built features, data-driven wedge discovery | Cons: slow to validate, builds more before proving demand
- Reuses: all existing enterprise features + voice_history.py analytics

### Approach C: Concierge manual first

Manually handle dad's email replies via voice, charge $16/month manually, validate 4 weeks of payment before automating.
- Effort: XS | Risk: Low | Pros: zero code, real payment validation fast | Cons: doesn't scale, manual work
- Reuses: none (no code)

## Recommended Approach

Approach B: Ideal architecture — progressive disclosure platform.

Rationale: All enterprise features already exist and are security-hardened (commit 8946a01). The gap is not code — it's wedge validation and distribution. Progressive disclosure lets users discover the platform through their primary workflow (voice email handling) while analytics reveals which other features resonate, avoiding the "build everything first" mistake that led to the current pre-product state.
